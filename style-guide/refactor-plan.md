# Refactor plan

The order of work for bringing the show onto the principles, with the two briefs in
`external-advice/`, the review of the filmed run and the notes in `general-feedback.md` folded in.
Each slide's own intended update lives in `show-intents.json` and is read in the organizer; this
file is the sequence, not the detail.

Sizes as in the completion plan: S an hour, M a session, L a long session, XL needs a decision or
data first. Phases 1 and 2 can run in parallel; everything after 3 depends on 1.

## Phase 0 — Decisions that code cannot take

Ask now, build meanwhile. Nothing in later phases is blocked on these except where marked XL.

- **Are the two walls continuous or separated?** Four backdrops draw one picture across both. If
  there is a gap between the surfaces, those four change shape.
- **Which company the figures belong to**: 210 miljoen against 1,4 miljard, and the six countries.
- **What the QR reaches**, so the ending's supporting line can be true. The headline is agreed:
  Ontdek The Circle. The Circle cannot be visited.
- **The real sectors**, with a project and a photograph each; the Dutch factories; the ladder's
  levels and the reason it changed; the figures behind both charts and the footprint bands; the
  mark as an svg; the two unreadable phrases (nul-loos processen, C4 Ontdoen van).

## Phase 1 — Foundations · L

The classes in `principles.md`, each extracted from the slide that already does it best rather
than written fresh, then adopted slide by slide in the later phases.

- **Palette, Scale, Frame** from the constants scattered through the drawers. Scale carries the
  projection: a pixel is 6.25 mm on a 12 m wall, the room sits 8 to 10 m away.
- **Leader** from the chart kit, replacing the four copies in the charts, the hidden story, the
  map and the analysis.
- **Grain** from the two concrete samplers, with a per-surface strength so saturated fills take
  less of it.
- **Arrive, Grow, Swap, Count** from the chart kit and the ladder. **Slots** from the ladder and the
  crowd. **Breathe** from the chapter card's light.
- **Clicks with their own length** inside one slide, so a long push and a short cull stop sharing
  one duration. The auto cues, the previews and the debug view follow.
- **A debug grid over the whole wall**, taken from the opening wall's ruled field with its small
  titles, drawn on the window like the debug overlay so it never lands in a still, switched from
  the organizer beside the concrete toggle. Once it exists it is the grid every layout is checked
  against, and possibly a layer some slides keep.
- **The guide brought up to date**: the frame as two separate walls, the geometry above, the
  three treatments, the chapter's shape (recap in, takeaway out), ambient motion as a category,
  export pacing against live pacing, and an on-site validation step in the workflow.

## Phase 2 — Copy and content · M

No drawers change; the show's lists do, and every one is checked in the organizer.

- The ending: Ontdek The Circle, no visit.
- One wording of the chapter 2 sentence on the programme and the quote; one spelling of the
  chapter 4 title everywhere.
- Dutch: compounds closed, sentence case through the stack, CO₂ everywhere, België, the phase
  headers and C4 corrected, the database label.
- One vocabulary for the production chain in chapters 3 and 4; Demonteren for taking apart.
- The hidden story's pieces matched to their claims.
- Six countries: Frankrijk in place of the second Luxemburg.
- Callouts of two or three words on the building. Bullets on the charts down to three, the rest
  to notes. Axis units and the indexed measure stated.
- The certificate's exit line. The domino's own title.
- The stack's last block blue rather than red.

## Phase 3 — The chapter's frame · L

What holds the chapters together, done before the slides inside them.

- **Chapter cards inverted**: light letters standing in a dark wall, the reverse of today.
- **Shadow that visibly moves**: slow but seen, as light falling across the wall. The card's
  breathing light is at the edge of noticing now; it comes up until it reads from the room, and
  the same setting goes on the shadow walls.
- **Less bare wall** at every chapter's opening.
- **A recap state** on each chapter's opening quote, and **a takeaway state** closing each chapter.
  Four of each; the four sentences come from the script work.
- Who is speaking: the pen keeps drawing on the right. The programme as the evening's menu, no
  wordmark. The belt arrives on a cut.

## Phase 4 — The slides, in running order · L, several sessions

Each slide takes the classes from Phase 1 and its own intended update. The notes from
`general-feedback.md` are in the intents; the ones that change a slide's shape are:

- **The catalogue city** opens a little further out and zooms in softly with a small pan.
- **The tree** highlights one thing on the first click, a second on the next, then fans them all.
- **De cijfers and the travelling slabs become one slide**: one block centred close up, a figure a
  click, the country list and the rest arriving as further clicks in the same run.
- **The factories** carry every Willy Naessens location, scraped from the contact page, factories
  as now and the rest as small blue dots; the sea is blue.
- **Geen compensatie** reveals the electrification steps one illustration a click once the
  certificate has gone.
- **The hidden story's** leader lands on the red level of the piece itself.
- **Demontabel in de stad** places each piece exactly on one roof and moves it to exactly one
  other; nothing drifts between.
- Then the rest as their intents say: the column's paragraph out of the band, the life cycle's
  columns, the recyclage's roles, an ending for chapter 3 in place of the turning panels, the
  webtool's callouts, the callback slide as chapter 4's takeaway.

## Phase 5 — The walls · L

- The yard: a tighter row and a shadow that reads on black, breathing.
- The shadows wall held for its three forms, a shorter bare seam, breathing instead of resetting.
- One block city, moved out of chapter 4 into a course.
- The gallery breathing; the closing scene on the opening's black.
- **The plain wall as a score**: its build is now an explicit list of reveals that exports as
  MIDI, so the wall's timing can drive its music. That is the direction; the placeholder values
  become The Circle's elements and the colour change slows to a wall's pace.
- **The sectors regrouped**: the floating pieces kept, given deliberate grouping, spacing,
  orientation and one focus; fewer sectors, each real.
- **The case studies as composed views** · L on its own: a sequence of framed views with
  precise transitions, drawing overview to facade detail and back, facts in clear margins.
- The ending laid out around the action, the code large and stable; the pen drawing the code
  tried once.

## Phase 6 — Sound · M

- Cue sheets for chapters 2 to 4, accents on meaningful events rather than an equal count.
- Four stings, or one voiced per chapter.
- Beds that fade across a chapter boundary instead of cutting; the loop ducking under marks.
- Sound for the ending and the exit.
- The plain wall's MIDI into the score for that course.

## Phase 7 — In the room · M, on site

- Film the full run once more and read it as before.
- Readability, contrast and timing on the projection; the QR from the back of the room.
- Settle the wall-continuity question by looking at it.
- Rehearse one chapter ending, one dinner hold and one return, with the host's readiness signal.

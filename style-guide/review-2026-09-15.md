`SLIDES_CUES=auto` — 809 s, 3840×1080, 60 fps — with the concrete over the whole frame. One frame every
4 s (202 frames) on contact sheets, then full-size frames wherever a sheet raised a question, measured
where a number settles it. The timeline below is read off the `.cues` log, which fires a cue on every

Everything is judged against `visual.md`, `motion.md` and `pacing.md`. Two things to keep in mind
reading it: the auto cues hold every state for its click plus 3,5 s (25 s for a wall), so some of what
reads as "too long" here is the export's pacing and not the show's; and the concrete is on, so the walls

## What reads well

The slides in the four chapters follow the guide closely: black pane, white type, red for the point
being made, one blue; titles centred at the top or top-left over a paragraph; type at the sizes the
guide gives (measured at 1:1 on the CO₂ charts, the analysis, the hidden story and the reduction
columns: titles 40 px, labels 26–32 px, body 30 px). Bars grow from their axis, rungs take their step,
text crossfades, the crowd re-forms, the recyclage stages travel left. The gallery on black with red
shadows, the belt, the opening and closing scenes and the programme wall are all as intended. No blended
frame between subjects on a cut, one exception below.

## Findings, in running order

Each one: what is seen and when, the rule it sits against, and the fix. **L** is a local change (one
drawer or one line of the show), **G** a change to the engine or the whole show.

### Walls and moments

1. **Who is speaking (0:24–0:49) — the right projector is black for 25 s.** The name stands at 0,095 of
   the height in the left pane; nothing on the right. Rule: a wide moment that carries type is two panes,
   one thing left, one right. **L:** put something on the right — the WN mark, or the element the opening
   wall is drawing at that moment — or set the name to the pane (0,14 of the height) so the wall is the
   name.

2. **Programme → Welcome (1:16) is a fade.** The list and "Het programma" dissolve into the belt over
   0,8 s (`ConveyorScene.transition = Fade`). Rule: cut by default; a fade only between two states of the
   same thing. **L:** `transition = Cut` on the belt, or keep the fade only where the belt follows the
   opening wall (black into black).

3. **Shadows wall (6:12–6:38) — the triangle is never seen.** The three forms take 12 s each, the wall
   was held 25 s: the circle and the square play, the triangle does not. Each turn also opens on about 3 s
   of a bare grid across both projectors (6:12, 6:24, 6:36), a quarter of the wall's time. **L:** the wall's
   `settle` should be `masks.size × period` (36 s) so the auto cues hold it for a full cycle, and `hold`
   could come down from 2 to 1,3 so the bare seam is shorter. **G:** see G16.

4. **Yard on black (8:43–9:08) is 97% black.** Measured at 8:48: one red piece at the right edge, the
   rest of the wall empty; the navy shadow (`12244A` where two lap) is nearly the ground's own value and
   does not read. The yard was laid out for a shadow to fall on white. **L:** keep the black, but tighten
   the row (`gap` 0,035 → ~0,015 and a smaller piece height so three or four pieces stand in view), and
   lift the shadow to a navy that reads on black (`wnBlue` for one layer, `2E4A8A` for the overlap).
   Measure the black share back to ~75%.

5. **Two block cities back to back inside chapter 4 (10:10–11:01), and the card replays.** Fifty seconds
   of wall mid-chapter; stepping forward out of the second one into *The Circle & ESG* counts as
   entering the section, so the chapter card reveals itself again and the sting fires a second time
   (the cue log: `1-01.wav` at 11:02). **L:** move both walls to the end of the chapter or into the
   *Vragen* moment, or archive one (the plain one is the house-colour version, the ruled one the
   drawing). **G:** see G17.

6. **De sectoren (11:22–11:42) — the caption is a slide label on a wall.** "01 / 03 Industriebouw,
   Wandpaneel met sparing" is set at about 0,03 of the height, at the left edge, while the sector is the
   subject. **L:** the sector name at 0,06 of the height, the element and the counter under it at 0,03.

7. **Case studies (11:44–12:10) — the facts lie on the drawings.** The drawings take 96% of the pane, so
   the name runs across the dimension lines at the top and the function list lies over the plan (Kievits
   II, 12:00). Rule: text never lies over a shape it does not belong to. **L:** `FIT` 0,96 → 0,80 with the
   facts in the band that leaves free, and the name at 0,055 rather than 0,04. Most facts are still empty
   in the source (known).

8. **Plain wall (12:11–12:34) — red to navy and back every 8 s.** The ground crosses a muddy purple twice
   a cycle and changes faster than any other wall in the evening. Rules: walls loop slowly (12 s to
   4 min) and never start or stop; flat fills. **L:** `period` 8 → 90 s, or hold one colour and swap it at
   the seam. The bar values are placeholders (known).

9. **Ending (12:36–13:02) — the action is small.** "Bouw mee. Scan en plan uw bezoek aan The Circle." is
   the one line the room is asked to act on and it is set at 0,04 of the height in red on black, the
   address at 0,026 in grey; the QR is 0,4 of the height. **L:** the action at the sentence's own size
   (or 0,06), the QR at 0,5, the address in white.

## Improvements to make

### Local (one drawer, one line)

In the order they pay off: 4 (yard), 3 (shadows hold), 10 (title pictures), 7 (blueprint facts),
9 (ending), 8 (plain period), 5 (block-city placement), 2 (belt cut), 1 (who is speaking), 6 (sectors
caption), 15, 14, 16, 12.

### Global (engine or whole show)

- **G16 — a wall is held for a whole loop.** In `autoCues`, a wide slide with one step should be held for
  `max(loop, holdWide)`, and a `ShadowFacade` with several masks should declare `settle = masks × period`.
  Then no export cuts a wall mid-cycle and the triangle plays.
- **G17 — a backdrop inside a chapter does not re-announce it.** In `Show.kt`, stepping forward out of a
  wide slide whose neighbours on both sides are in the same section should not count as entering the
  section: no card replay, no sting. Or the organizer refuses a backdrop inside a chapter, and the order
  file keeps them between chapters and in moments.
- **G18 — title pictures generated from the chapter names.** Set the title with the deck's own `setToFit`
  into the mask the facade reads (the typeset card already paints a plate), with breaks between words and
  after hyphens, in the v2 face and size. Adding or renaming a chapter then needs no png, and the breaks
  follow the rule by construction. `SLIDES_CARD_TITLES` stays for a hand-drawn override.
- **G19 — click lengths per step.** `Slide.stepFrames(step)` beside `stepFrames`, so a slide with one long
  push and one short cull (the city), or a build-down slower than its reveal, states each length; the
  debug view, the auto cues and the organizer previews follow. Today every click of a slide is one
  length.
- **G20 — the light over the whole wall.** The lamp term added to the card this session could sit in the
  concrete overlay in `Show.kt` as well, at a fifth of the card's strength (±5%) and on the same slow
  clock, so every still slide and wall shows the projection breathing the way the card now does. One
  toggle beside `SLIDES_CONCRETE_ON`; off for stills and previews like the concrete itself. Worth a filmed
  minute before deciding: the guide's flat fills are the reason to keep it faint.
- **G21 — a size floor for type on the walls.** The walls set their captions at slide sizes (0,03–0,04 of
  the height) while a wall carries one thing read across a room: the sectors, the case studies, the
  ending's action, the speaker's name. One constant for "a caption on a wall" (≈0,055) in `Type.kt`,
  read by the wall drawers, keeps them alike.

## Done in this session: the chapter title's light moves

`ShadowFacade` gained two terms, both functions of `stage.frame` and both 0 on the walls: `breathe`
lifts the sun 10° off its resting 40° and settles it back every 45 s once the reveal is over, so the
shadow filling the letters lengthens and shortens; and `glow` is a lamp wandering over the wall — a
broad soft spot on a path that does not retrace itself, once across in 70 s — that leaves the lit faces
30% darker away from it than under it, scaling only what the sun lit so the shadows stay black. Set on
`chapterCard` in `Slideshow.kt`; documented in CLAUDE.md under the Shadows card.

Checked on a filmed 40 s of chapter 1, `video/card-light-check.mp4`: the mean brightness of the card's
left, middle and right thirds went 137 / 166 / 187 at 6 s to 153 / 178 / 196 at 28 s — the light is seen
to cross the wall while the words stay read. Not yet looked at in the room: whether 0,3 is too much under
the projector's own falloff. The strength is one number.

## Content: what I would change in the copy

Read off `Slideshow.kt` rather than the frames. Wording first, then what the slides say.

**Two versions of one sentence.** The programme wall says "ESG is geen checklijst, maar de manier
waarop…" and the chapter 2 quote says "ESG is geen checklist, maar de systemische manier waarop…". One
of them, on both; and "systematische" is probably the word meant.

**The chapter title is written three ways.** `chapter("The circle: Een nieuwe manier van denken")`
against the programme's "The Circle: een nieuwe manier van denken" and the v3 picture's capitals. The
running order, the organizer and the typeset fallback all print the Kotlin one.

**Sentence case and compounds, Dutch.** The stack: "Eigen grond en- omgevingswerken" (the hyphen has
moved: "grond- en omgevingswerken"), "Eigen Montageploegen", "Eigen Dakdichtingsbedrijf", "Eigen
AluSchrijn- en Glasbedrijf" beside "Eigen transport" and "Eigen prefabricatie" — one case for the list.
The life cycle: "Grondstof winning", "fabricage plek", "constructie plek" are one word each; "B4-B5
Gebruiksfase" heads a column that runs B1 to B5; "C4 Ontdoen van" is cut short (C4 is *verwijdering*).
The analysis: "Co2 impact" should go through `setLine` as CO₂-impact like every other CO₂ on the wall,
"aangekocht materialen" is "aangekochte", and "Levenscyclus analyse" is one word (the levers slide
already writes it so). The column: "Energie productie" is one word, and "Bindmiddelen (cement,
bewapening etc.)" puts reinforcement among the binders — "Cement (bindmiddel)" is what the 80% is.
The levers: "nul-loos processen" is not a phrase — WN's word is needed (afvalloos? zonder lozing?).
"BELGIE" is "BELGIË".

**The same chain in two vocabularies.** Chapter 3 says Grondstofwinning · Transport naar fabricageplek ·
Fabricage · Transport naar constructieplek · Installatie; chapter 4 says Extractie ruw materiaal ·
Transport naar productiefabriek · Productie van een product · Transport van product naar constructieplek ·
Constructie, which reads as a translation. One list for both: Grondstofwinning · Transport naar de
fabriek · Productie · Transport naar de bouwplaats · Montage. And "Uit elkaar halen bouwblokken" is
"Demonteren", the word the same chapter uses twice already.

**The pieces do not match their claims on the hidden story.** The TANDBALK carries "−30% CO₂ in
gevelelementen", the WAND "−15% in gewelven", the X-BALK "−20% in gewapend beton". A facade element is
a wall panel and a gewelf is a floor: WAND_27 for the first, a TT plate or hollow-core for the second,
a beam for the third, so the picture says what the figure says.

**Six countries, five named.** The slab lists LUXEMBURG twice; the factory list has Nancy, so the
sixth is FRANKRIJK. And the factory list is thirteen Belgian and French sites with not one in the
Netherlands, under a speaker from Willy Naessens Nederland — the meeting asked for NL + BE. Den Bosch
is on the map as a city with no factory near it.

**The figures are split across two entities.** "210 miljoen omzet" on the chapter 1 slabs against
€ 1,4 miljard in the meeting: one is the Dutch company and one the group, and the slide should say
which — the audience will know the difference.

**The certificate's own words.** It reads "Compensatie CO₂ emissies" and leaves; the slide's title is
"Geen compensatie". That works as a story, but the sting is silent: a line on the certificate's exit
("Niet compenseren. Reduceren.") would land the point the notes make.

**Callouts that are sentences.** "Bouwstenen kunnen op voorraad gemaakt worden" and "Automatische
generatie van CO₂ in gebouw" on the building; a callout is two or three words: "Bouwstenen op
voorraad", "CO₂ per gebouw, automatisch".

**Twelve sectors, three real.** Nine of the sectors on the collage are proposals and only three are
taken full frame. Twelve labelled sectors with no project behind nine of them is a promise the wall
cannot keep; six with a photograph each is the better wall. WN's list decides.

**Case-study words.** "10.000 m2 warenhuis" is a department store in Dutch; a logistics hall is a
*magazijn* or *distributiecentrum*, and "24 vrachtwagen bays" is "24 laaddocks". The name "Kievits II"
is spelt "kvitis" in the file names — check which is right.

**The ending is the chapter 4 quote again.** The first sentence of the quote is the ending's sentence.
As a callback that is fine; as the last thing on the wall it could be the one line the evening was
building to, and the action under it is a placeholder.

**The ending and the exit are silent.** The cue log releases the dessert bed at the ending and plays
nothing after it: the last fifty seconds of the evening, the call to action and the closing wall, have
no sound. A bed for the exit, and the ending's own cue.

## The five themes, and what each comes to

**Sound.** Chapter 1 has a mark on every slide (1-02 to 1-10); chapters 2 to 4 have only the sting
and the base loop. A cue sheet for the other three chapters, at the rate chapter 1 has: an arrival mark
per slide family (a chart, a piece in the round, the city), click marks where things are counted (the
life-cycle columns, the ladder's rungs, the reduction steps). The sting is the same file on all four
cards; four stings, or one pitched per chapter, would tell the chapters apart by ear. The base loop
under the slides could duck under each mark rather than sit at one level. The beds hand over with a
cut at every wall boundary; a bed that fades over the card's reveal (2,5 s out, 6 s in, as the guide
says) would join the courses to the chapters. And the exit wants sound (above).

**One coherent system.** The slides already are one: black pane, Rockwell, red and one blue. The walls
are not: on black the belt, the gallery, the yard and the plain wall; on concrete grey the cards, the
shadows and the ruled city; on paper the plain city and the closing scene. Two families would be
enough — the house pair on black for the catalogue walls, the concrete grey for the relief walls —
and the two paper walls are the ones to move (the plain city to navy on black, the closing scene to
the opening's black with the colours swapped). The other seam is the face: the chapter titles are a
grotesque and everything else is Rockwell, and with v3 the two now meet at title size on every
chapter — generating the title pictures from the chapter names in Rockwell Bold (G18) closes it.
The concrete at `mix = 2.5` mottles the belt's and the plain wall's saturated fills more than the
cards; 1,8 on the walls would keep the grain and lose the mud.

**Typography and layout.** The chart slides measure right. What is off is on the walls: captions at
slide sizes (the sectors, the case studies, the ending's action, the speaker's name) and text over
drawings (the blueprints). One caption size for walls (G21), the facts off the drawings, and the
paragraph measure held to 0,29 of the width everywhere a paragraph stands (the ladder's is; the
column's paragraph sits in the band).

**Programme and Ending.** The programme is clean but the list is quiet (moments at 0,026, grey) and the
right pane is a numeral and a sentence. Two ways to make it more: the right pane shows the chapter's
own picture — its title cut into the facade, so the programme previews the cards — with the message
under it; or the list is set as the menu, the four chapters large with the courses as rules between
them, and the right pane holds one message at a time. The ending: the action as the headline and the QR
at half the pane (finding 9), and one idea worth a try — the code drawn by the opening wall's pen, one
line travelling its modules and flooding, so the evening closes the way it opened.

**Backdrops.** In order of effect: the yard's density and shadow on black (4); the shadows wall held for
its three forms (3); one block city in chapter 4, not two (5); the plain wall's cycle slowed to a
wall's pace (8); and each course wall tied to the chapter it follows, which they nearly are — the
gallery after the catalogue chapter, the shadows after the values, the yard after the concrete, and the
plain wall after The Circle should carry The Circle's elements rather than a placeholder chart.
